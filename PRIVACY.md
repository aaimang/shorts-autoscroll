# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 4 November 2026_

Shorts Autoscroll does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

## What the extension stores

Your settings only: whether autoscroll is on, the mode (when a Short ends or on a timer), the number of plays, the timer length and two on/off options. They are saved with Chrome's `storage.sync`, so Chrome may sync them between your own signed-in browsers. They never reach the developer or anyone else.

## What the extension reads

On `www.youtube.com`, the extension checks the page you are already viewing. It looks at the Shorts video player's playback time, whether the comments panel is open and the page address. It uses these only to decide when to move to the next Short. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

## Network requests

None. The extension has no servers, analytics or tracking, and it loads no remote code.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at https://github.com/aaimang/shorts-autoscroll/issues.
